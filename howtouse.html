How to Use MetroMatch

Using MetroMatch to plan your itinerary is a snap! Just follow these steps:

Getting Started

To get started, zoom in or out (use two fingers or mouse wheel) to areas of interest. Labels will get larger as you zoom in. The base map includes popular tourist attractions, e.g. the Smithsonian American Art Museum, shown with "museum" icons.

Zoom In

Zoom Out

Focus on a Station or Line

Focus on a station or line using the search bars atop the landing page. Click in the search bars to identify stations or lines, or zoom in or out to view neighborhoods (use a mouse wheel or two fingers on touch screens).

Search by Station

Search by Line

Using the Legend

Use the legend (top right) to toggle on/off destination types:

  • Pharmacies
  • Grocery stores
  • Nightlife
  • Coffee shops
  • Restaurants
  • Hotels

The map will display one or more options for each destination type as you click. Click again to remove a destination type. After selecting a destination, you will see the business name, nearest station and distance from the station.

Example: Destination details display

Booking Lodging

When you click on a lodging, you will see the hotel name, nearest station and distance from the station, along with links to three travel booking companies that allow you to explore prices and book your stay.

Example: Hotel details with booking options

Selecting and Saving Favorites

To select and save favorites, click on the star icon that appears when you select a destination. Then click on the star in the legend to display your choices. Click again on your destinations, then the star, to remove.

Legend with favorites star icon

Destinations by Station

The Destinations by Station tab provides a tabular list of amenities by each station. Click to expand each window.

Destinations organized by station

Itineraries

The Itineraries tab features our recommendations for full or half-day experiences traveling in the DMV (District of Columbia-Maryland-Virginia) entirely by Metrorail. We focus on stops that highlight the District's African American heritage and the region's immigrant culture, independent businesses, historic districts, and vibrant neighborhoods.

Curated Metro itineraries

Planning Your Trip

Click the WMATA Trip Planner (top left) to plan your trip by transit, which will open in a new window.
