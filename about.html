Visit DC Car-Free

Are you coming to Washington DC for work or on vacation? Do you need a place to stay? Are you searching for destinations to eat, drink, shop, and celebrate? MetroMatch has you covered. Browse our database of hundreds of hotels, restaurants, museums, cafes, and nightlife within walking distance from Metro, MARC, and VRE. Lower your carbon footprint, save money, time, and hassle, and experience the region like a local for a trip to remember.

Why Metro Match?

The MetroMatch platform guides travelers – vacationers as well as hometown tourists – looking to take transit where they want to go. A leap beyond what you'll find in Google maps, MetroMatch provides a more curated experience, displaying all the places you can be as you plan your travel by train.

  • Seeking downtown's hottest experiences? MetroMatch is ideal for finding hotels and restaurants in the buzziest parts of town.
  • Traveling on a budget? See what's available to eat and drink and where to stay in the close-in suburbs, just a few stops from DC's famed museums.
  • Looking for a city adventure? MetroMatch helps you travel to neighborhoods that locals love and tourists often miss. And no more worries about parking fees!

Beyond those benefits, by using MetroMatch, you contribute to a more sustainable region by helping lower carbon emissions, reduce congestion and support local businesses.

About the Team

David Schneider is a data scientist, storyteller, activist, teacher, and founder of Schneider Analytics, which builds digital tools for the public good. Prior to starting his own business, he worked for over twenty years as a career civil servant with the Federal Transit Administration. David grew up in Washington DC and lives near the Silver Spring Metro station.

Valerie Berton has worked in the communications field for more than three decades, most recently directing a public affairs team at the Federal Transit Administration. Now she provides writing, editing and marketing support to organizations to help develop messages and stories that resonate, including promoting the many benefits of taking transit. She lives in Silver Spring, MD, less than two miles from the Metro station and 600 yards from a bus stop.

Privacy Statement

MetroMatch does not share personal data or location information about users. Our website and app uses your device's location solely to display your current position on the map and to enhance your experience by showing nearby transit stations and amenities. Location data is processed locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers or any third-party services. The MetroMatch team reserves the right to use emails for limited promotional use.
