Visit DC Car-Free

Are you coming to Washington DC for work or on vacation?? Do you need a place to stay? Are you searching for destinations to eat, drink, shop, and celebrate? DMV Transit Tourism has you covered. Browse our database of hundreds of hotels, restaurants, cafes, and nightlife within walking distance from Metro, MARC, and VRE. Lower your carbon footprint, save money, time, and hassle, and experience the region like a local for an Inauguration weekend to remember.

Data by the People, for the People

DMV Transit Tourism is built on information from OpenStreetMap (OSM) is a collaborative project that creates a free, editable map of the world, powered by a global community of contributors. OSM provides detailed, up-to-date geographic data contributed and maintained by people like you. This open-data initiative aims to empower individuals, organizations, and communities by making geographic information accessible to all.

About the Content Creator

David Schneider (he/him/his) is a data scientist, storyteller, activist, teacher, and founder of Schneider Analytics, which builds digital tools for the public good. Prior to starting his own business, he worked for over twenty years as a career civil servant with the Federal Transit Administration. David grew up in Washington DC and lives near the Silver Spring Metro station.
